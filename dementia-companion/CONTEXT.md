# Domain Glossary

## CaregiverAccount
A registered user of the caregiver dashboard. Owns one or more Patients.

## Patient
The person living with dementia who interacts with the Companion via a paired Tablet. Belongs to a CaregiverAccount.

## Companion
The AI persona configured for a Patient. Has a name, voice, personality preset, Portrait, and Avatar Assets. One Companion per Patient.

## Portrait
An AI-generated still image of the Companion's appearance. The Caregiver selects one from three generated options during onboarding. Required before Avatar Assets can be generated.

## Avatar Assets
The pair of resources — idle loop video and intro audio — generated in the background after Portrait selection. Both must be present before Introduction can proceed. The idle loop video is the Companion's animated resting state; the intro audio is the Companion's first-meeting greeting.

## Introduction
The one-time event where the Caregiver previews the Companion's Avatar Assets (watches the idle loop video, hears the intro audio) and approves the avatar. Approval and introduction are the same action — there is no intermediate "approved but not yet introduced" state. Triggers the Tablet to transition from Orb to the live Companion avatar and play the intro audio. Recorded as `avatarUnlocked = true` on the Companion.

## Orb
The placeholder shown on the Tablet before Introduction. A glowing circle displaying the Companion's initial letter.

## Pairing
The process of binding a Tablet device to a Patient. The Caregiver generates a Pairing Code in the caregiver dashboard; the Tablet enters it to receive a Device Token. Independent from Introduction — Pairing and Introduction can occur in either order.

## Pairing Code
An 8-character code generated by the Caregiver, valid for 10 minutes, single-use. Consumed on successful Pairing.

## Device Token
A server-issued opaque token stored on the Tablet after Pairing. Authenticates all Tablet-to-server requests.

## Memory Card
A structured piece of patient context — a person, place, topic, or behavioral note — that the Companion draws on to personalize conversation.

## Conversation
A single voice session between a Patient and the Companion, with a start time, optional end time, transcript turns, and an AI-inferred mood score.

## Safety Event
A moment of patient distress or risk detected during a Conversation, classified by severity and trigger type. Notified to the Caregiver via push or SMS.
